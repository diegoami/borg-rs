# Death review: brainstorming and overall assessment

Required when processing a natural-death report or materially updating its
analysis. Copy into the dossier or complete the same sections in report.md;
link from assessment.json and the case investigation. The initial exporter can
create an unassessed dossier; processing is incomplete until this review is
written, or explicitly marked provisional with missing evidence and next work.
Do not manufacture conclusions to fill the template. Shared encounter families
can reference one review while retaining each actual build/evidence identity.

## Overall picture

State the character/build, what happened, the earliest potentially consequential
choices, the fatal sequence, and what is known versus unresolved. Explain how
stock, threat valuation, route knowledge, action order and recovery interact.
Separate immediate cause, possible contributing mechanisms and alternatives.

Include pre-exposure, entry/teleport, first legal retreat decision, last escape
consumption, and later opportunities before death where relevant. World ticks
are not all player decisions. Hidden diagnostic state is not Borg knowledge.

## Brainstorming and candidate fit

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Consider preparedness/restock, economic versus lethal danger, movement/door/
upstairs routes, attempted walking escape, action priority, healing/defense,
implementation/harness faults and ordinary bad luck as relevant. Do not require
one issue for every category or presume a bug. Search existing issues first.
Attempted escape must be assessed especially after consumable exhaustion; a
route need not be guaranteed safe to compare it with staying/fighting. If a move
is illegal or blocked, show the obstruction. Distinguish a possible attempt,
a known complete route and a proved successful escape.

## Priorities and next evidence

Rank focused investigations, explain dependencies and select the next test that
separates explanations. Keep shared/alternative causes distinct, so combined
changes do not hide which helps. Bugs remain first. State what is deferred,
rejected or inconclusive and why. Link owner observations and answer them with
findings; acknowledgement alone does not resolve a candidate.

## Outcome and limits

Summarise proved facts, unproved rescue claims and the proposed path forward.
Update feedback/actions and regenerate the registry. Implementation experiments
follow [the C-consumer adoption contract](../C-CONSUMER-ADOPTION.md),
with separately declared survival and long-term-progress evidence. Neither brainstorm nor diagnostic rescue proves
population benefit. No strategy change starts from completing this review.
