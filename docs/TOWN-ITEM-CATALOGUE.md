# Town inventory: item catalogue

Owner review · phase 4 shadow mode · **v5 trip-relative utility**.

**The old fixed stock targets are replaced.** The table below maps items to units of utility; it is not a shopping list or a minimum kit. The [model specification](TOWN-UTILITY.md) gives the equations, assumptions and limitations. The [offline page](town-item-catalogue.html) adds search.

## What determines a purchase now

| Need | Supply unit | Utility depends on | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Sources | Trip duration relative to approximate torch lifetime; decreasing value of spares | 100 |
| Food | Ration-equivalents | Expected trip duration; substitutes share coverage | 100 |
| Healing | HP, capped at max HP per use | HP, duration and destination depth relative to character level | 100 |
| Poison | Cure for the current condition | Poisoned now, with no alternative cure | 100 |
| Escape | Uses | Duration and destination depth relative to character level | 50 |
| Trip / Recall | Uses | Walking avoided on the intended journey; shallow return on foot competes | 50 |
| Power | Positive gear/tool power difference | Intended depth advancement; consumables get no inventory-power bonus | 50 |
| Knowledge | First unknown copy | Learning a kind; no benefit from repeated copies of that kind within one shared supply option | 25 |
| Retained gold | Gold left after the entire plan | Increasing marginal cost as the remaining purse shrinks; no fixed reserve fraction | 25 |

Destination is explicit `borg_town_target_depth`, or the next frontier (max depth + 1). Duration is explicit `borg_town_trip_turns`, or an exploration-and-walking forecast. These settings declare ambition without setting item quantities. Utility uses thousandths in v5 records.

Keeping an item competes with storing it for later. The Home retains discounted future supply utility, including shallow-trip Recall, even when the pack has room. Ordinary purchases compete with retaining cash. Current urgent conditions still take precedence; there are no floors.

**Remaining gaps:** fuel, books, ammunition, diggers and devices do not all have direct purchase mappings. Food/lifetime/exposure forecasts are approximate policy rates, not measured survival probabilities. The planner remains observation-only.
