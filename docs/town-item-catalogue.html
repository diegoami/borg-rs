Town inventory: item catalogue

Owner review · phase 4 shadow mode · v5 trip-relative utility

The fixed food, healing, escape and Recall stock targets are replaced. Each row identifies the supply unit; purchases compare marginal trip utility with carrying burden and retaining gold. The Home can retain supplies for future trips.

Destination is explicit borg_town_target_depth, or max depth + 1. Duration is explicit borg_town_trip_turns, or an exploration-and-walking forecast. Healing uses HP rather than equal potion counts. Recall is valued by walking avoided, with no two-scroll requirement. Utility has decreasing marginal returns and substitutes share coverage.

Weights remain configurable priorities. Food/lifetime/exposure rates are estimates, not measured survival probabilities. See the model specification and the GitHub-rendered table. The planner is still observation-only.

Scope: named ordinary stock, town books, and possible Black Market allocations. Random generation filters determine actual availability. Gold rows show remaining mapping gaps; the Home's urgent-source gap remains open.
